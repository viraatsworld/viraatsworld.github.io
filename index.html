How I work

From dataset to deployed robot

I own the whole loop — and the loop closes on the real robot, not in a notebook.

  1. Capture data

    Datasets from recorded rosbags and synthetic data from simulation.

    rosbags · RealSense · Isaac Sim

  2. Train

    Detection and pose models, and RL policies with shaped rewards and curricula.

    PyTorch · YOLOv8 · SAC/PPO

  3. Validate in sim

    Repeatable testing before hardware trials, with domain randomization.

    Isaac Sim · MuJoCo · Gazebo

  4. Plan & orchestrate

    Motion planning plus Behavior Trees for execution and failure recovery.

    MoveIt · CuRobo · BTs

  5. Deploy on the cell

    Hand-eye calibration, bring-up and live inference on real arms.

    ros2_control · Docker · CI/CD
