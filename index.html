RL policies (SAC/PPO) for contact-rich pick-and-place inside a confined work cell, trained in Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab and MuJoCo with reward shaping, curricula and domain randomization. The sim-to-real side covers system identification, observation noise and latency modelling.
